# Every demo video, in order

Nothing has been deleted: each version made during the hackathon is kept here under its own name. All are silent
(record your voice-over on top). "en" / "ar" is the language of the on-screen captions.

| # | File | Length | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | `00-placeholder-skeleton-30s.mp4` (+ .gif) | 30 s | First pipeline test on the empty app skeleton. Not a real demo; kept for completeness. |
| 01 | `01-emulator-recording-30s-first-cut.mp4` (+ .gif) | 30 s | First screen recording of the working app on an Android 14 emulator: scam SMS held, incident, Not a scam, call rejected, report. |
| 02 | `02-emulator-recording-30s-final.mp4` (+ .gif) | 30 s | Same storyboard, re-recorded on the final build. |
| 03 | `03-real-phone-explainer-15s.mp4` | 15 s | Explainer recorded on a real Android 15 phone (made by a team session). |
| 04 | `04-slides-explainer-20s-en-with-footer.mp4`, `…-ar-with-footer.mp4` (+ .gif) | 20 s | Captioned explainer built from real screenshots, one step per scene, with a footer line. |
| 05 | `05-slides-explainer-20s-en.mp4`, `…-ar.mp4` (+ .gif) | 20 s | Same explainer without the footer line. |
| 06 | `06-live-two-phones-17s-en.mp4`, `…-ar.mp4` (+ .gif) | 17 s | Live recording of two emulators side by side: same scam SMS and call, without Mirsad (left) and with Mirsad (right). |
| 07 | `07-hyperframes-animated-20s-en.mp4`, `…-ar.mp4` | 20 s | Animated demo built with HyperFrames from the app's screens and flow: the SMS arrives and is held, the reasons appear, the scammer's call is rejected before it rings, Not a scam restores a message, the report opens. Drawn, not recorded. Source: `../hyperframes/`. |

The files linked from the main README (`../mirsad-android-demo*.mp4`, `../mirsad-explainer-15s.mp4`) are copies of
versions 03, 06 and 07.

Voice-over scripts: `../../docs/VOICEOVER.md` (written for the 20 s explainer and the 17 s live demo; for 07 the same
steps apply, with the call line starting at about 10.8 s).
