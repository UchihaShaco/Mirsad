# Mirsad build: decisions made during the build

The owner asked for routine, reversible decisions to be made without approval rounds. This file lists every ruling recorded in the build ledger, in the order it was made, each with what it costs if it was wrong, followed by the items deliberately left open. The design notes (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-mirsad-design.md`) and the README carry the resulting behaviour; this file explains why.

- Disclosed replies and officer handoff (2026-11-22). The local assistant identifies itself in its first delivered reply, answers identity questions honestly, and may leave an ordinary exchange unanswered. Automated replies require an explicitly approved chat and recorded consent in either intake scope. Wait at least three seconds after the latest incoming activity. A contact may opt out of replies to their own chat. Only explicit re-enrollment from that contact restores consent. A shared document or concrete location opens a message-linked human review and holds automated replies until each outstanding item is reviewed. The first startup of this version skips the existing reply queue and honors historical contact preferences.

- Telegram verification for automated replies (2026-11-22). The owner approved keeping **Read Messages** enabled alongside the required **Reply to Messages** permission. The setup check accepts this pair for reply-enabled connections. Other granted rights still fail verification, and intake-only verification accepts zero grants. Mirsad never marks a message as read.

- Standalone bot-or-human questions (2026-11-22). The owner chose to leave these turns unanswered. This includes an opening question. The first delivered reply still identifies Mirsad, questions about the service's operation or privacy receive an honest answer, and a skipped turn does not opt the contact out.

- Contact-initiated reopening (2026-11-22). A fresh invitation from an enrolled contact can reopen their own conversation. A greeting or direct question is enough after a plain stop. A stronger no-contact request requires a deliberate invitation. Stops cancel pending replies. The worker starts the model history at the new invitation and continues honoring all evidence-review and officer holds.

- Natural openings in enrolled test chats (2026-11-23). The owner removed the first-reply project introduction. A simple greeting gets a greeting. A direct identity question gets a brief truthful answer. The app omits earlier branded outgoing introductions from model input and records whether fixed wording or the local model produced each new reply. Contact preferences, officer controls and evidence-review holds continue to govern delivery.

- Model-written replies throughout (2026-11-23). The owner asked to remove the fixed reply path because repeated wording sounded scripted. Every eligible turn reaches local Qwen with recent conversation history. It can clarify an ambiguous fragment, give a concise truthful answer to a direct question, or stay silent after an answer. Exact repeated identity answers go through the guard. Case history keeps the wording source originally recorded for each send.

- Conversation about offers (2026-11-23). An ordinary offer in an enrolled chat may get a brief acknowledgment or one relevant question about the work, item or service. A later request for money is interpreted in light of terms already supplied. The prompt includes synthetic examples in both languages. Evidence intake continues to preserve items the contact shares voluntarily.

- Job fees and tone (2026-11-23). The owner chose a short question about the purpose of an unexplained fee to start work. Once the sender explains the fee, acknowledge it and leave the next turn to them. Match the sender’s English or Arabic register and keep wording understated. The model writes each reply.

- Shared material (2026-11-23). The first distinct payment link, document or relevant location supplied by the enrolled contact is immediately visible on the officer portal. The second distinct item pauses replies for review. When the sender volunteers payment-recipient bank details, the first such item immediately pauses replies. Each flag links to its source. An offer, an amount and an explanation of a fee do not count as shared material.
